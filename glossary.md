# Translation rules / 翻譯規則

Read by the local Qwen translator (added to its prompt) and by Claude when
asked to retranslate a video. Edit this file in the repo, then copy it to
`~/.diolingo/glossary.md`, which is the copy actually used.

## Keep in English / 保持英文，不翻譯

- Companies and brands: Tesla, SpaceX, BYD, Toyota, Hyundai, Morgan Stanley,
  Unitree, Figure, Nvidia, Apple, Uber
- Products and services: Model 3, Model Y, Model S, Model X, Cybertruck,
  Cybercab, Robotaxi (Tesla's service), Tesla Semi / Semi, Optimus,
  Megacharger, Supercharger, Starlink, Grok, Raptor, Dojo
- Technology and abbreviations: FSD, Autopilot, AI4, AI5, 4680, OTA, AEB,
  TAM, TCO, EBITDA, EPA, NHTSA, IIHS, Level 2, PT (price target), CEO
- Places and plants: Gigafactory, Giga Texas, Giga Berlin, Giga Nevada,
  Fremont, Austin, Silicon Valley
- People: keep every personal name in English (Elon Musk / Elon, Jason
  Calacanis, Brett Adcock, ...). Do not write 馬斯克.
- Shows and media: All-In podcast, X (the platform)

## Translate / 翻成中文

- humanoid robot → 人形機器人
- EV / electric vehicle → 電動車; hybrid → 油電混合車
- robotaxi as a generic concept (not Tesla's) → 無人計程車
- price target → 目標價; bull case → 樂觀情境; base case → 基本情境;
  equal weight → 中性評級; gross margin → 毛利率; market cap → 市值
- autonomous trucking → 自駕卡車; self-driving → 自動駕駛
- Numbers, units and currency stay as written: $840, 550 km, 40 t, 800 kW,
  293 miles, 10%

## Style / 風格

- Traditional Chinese as used in Taiwan, spoken tone (像在說話，不要書面翻譯腔).
  Never use Simplified characters.
- Full-width punctuation (，。、？！), and a half-width space between Chinese
  and any English word or number: 「Tesla 在 Giga Nevada 生產 Semi」.
- One English cue → one Chinese line. Never merge, split or complete a
  fragment; consecutive lines just need to read naturally in sequence.
- Sound tags: [Music] → [音樂], [Applause] → [掌聲], [Laughter] → [笑聲].
- No notes, no parentheses with explanations, no added words.

## Examples / 範例

- "Tesla is building the Semi in Giga Nevada" → 「Tesla 在 Giga Nevada 生產 Semi」
- "Morgan Stanley raised its bull case to $840" → 「Morgan Stanley 把樂觀情境目標價上調到 $840」
- "Optimus can already cook an egg" → 「Optimus 已經會煎蛋了」
